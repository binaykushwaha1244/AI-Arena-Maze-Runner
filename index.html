How to Play AI Arena

1

Pick an Algorithm & Watch Pseudocode

Choose BFS, DFS, Greedy, or A*. As the robot explores, the Pseudocode panel highlights each step (dequeue, walk, backtrack, explore) in real time.

2

Customize the Arena or Race the AI

Click & drag to draw custom walls or clear open fields. Toggle Race me and steer the yellow dot with WASD or arrow keys to beat the robot!

3

Switch to "Be the Algorithm" Game Mode

Test your intuition: choose which waiting cell the algorithm must visit next according to its data structure rule. Earn score streaks for correct picks!
